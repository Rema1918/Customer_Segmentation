# Customer Persona Summary

## Overview
Customer segmentation is performed using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) analysis with the K-Means clustering algorithm. The resulting clusters represent customers with different purchasing behaviours.

## Customer Persona Types

### 1. Loyal High-Value Customers
- Purchase frequently and spend more.
- Have purchased relatively recently.
- **Suggested strategy:** Offer loyalty rewards, exclusive discounts, and personalized recommendations.

### 2. Frequent Customers
- Place orders regularly.
- May contribute consistently to overall sales.
- **Suggested strategy:** Provide repeat-purchase offers and loyalty benefits.

### 3. High-Spending Customers
- Have a relatively high total purchase value.
- May make fewer but higher-value purchases.
- **Suggested strategy:** Recommend relevant premium products and bundles.

### 4. At-Risk or Inactive Customers
- Have not purchased recently.
- May need additional engagement to encourage another purchase.
- **Suggested strategy:** Use targeted re-engagement campaigns and relevant offers.

### 5. Occasional Customers
- Purchase less frequently than regular customers.
- May have potential to become repeat customers.
- **Suggested strategy:** Send relevant product recommendations and purchase reminders.

## Important Note
These are possible persona categories, not confirmed results. Actual personas must be validated using the cluster summary generated by `Customer_Segmentation.ipynb`. Not every category will necessarily appear in the final clusters.

## Conclusion
Customer personas help businesses understand purchasing patterns and plan suitable marketing, retention, and loyalty strategies. The final persona descriptions should reflect the actual Recency, Frequency, and Monetary values of each cluster.
